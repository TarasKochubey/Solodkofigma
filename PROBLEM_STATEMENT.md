## User
People who use insulin daily (primary) and must count carbohydrates at every meal as part of their personal health management — using injections, insulin pens, or an insulin pump.

Secondary: People with other conditions requiring precise daily carbohydrate tracking.

---

## The Core Problem
Every meal for a person who uses insulin requires a real-time carbohydrate count. This count directly informs their daily health management decisions. Estimating incorrectly creates anxiety, erodes confidence, and has real consequences for how they feel physically throughout the day.

This calculation is:
- **Time-pressured** — the food is in front of them right now
- **Cognitively demanding** — complex or homemade meals involve multiple ingredients with different carb profiles
- **Emotionally taxing** — the daily obligation adds to the existing weight of managing a long-term condition
- **Error-prone when guessed** — visual estimation of carb values is common, uncertain, and undermines confidence

For high-fat meals (pizza, pastry, fatty dishes), users must also account for delayed carbohydrate absorption, adding kilocalorie tracking on top of carb counting — dramatically increasing complexity.

---

## Why Existing Solutions Fail

| Category | Problem |
|---|---|
| General nutrition apps (MyFitnessPal, Cronometer) | Designed for calorie counting and weight management. Not built for carb precision at the meal moment. Complex interfaces add cognitive load. |
| Medical diabetes apps | Overly clinical. Hospital UX. Add cognitive burden rather than reducing it. Feel like a chore rather than support. |
| Manual calculation / visual estimation | Unreliable. Common default when apps fail. Creates uncertainty and anxiety. |
| Food label reading | Slow, requires mental math per gram eaten. No help for homemade or restaurant meals. |

---

## Current Workarounds
- Visual carb estimation ("eyeballing" — frequent and dangerous)
- General food tracking apps not built for insulin dosing
- Printed carb reference tables or books
- Asking family members, dietitians, or online communities
- Strict meal repetition to avoid recalculating (limits food freedom)

---

## Emotional Cost
- Feeling "not like everyone else" at restaurants and social meals
- Stress and anxiety at every meal
- Embarrassment of needing to calculate before eating
- Fatigue from the relentless daily obligation

---

## Cost of Inaction
- Carb estimation uncertainty → anxiety, worse daily health outcomes, reduced quality of life
- Chronic cognitive and emotional load → worse long-term adherence and wellbeing
- Reduced quality of life → dietary restriction, avoidance of social eating, mental health strain
- Users default to guessing → increased uncertainty and eroded confidence in daily decisions

---

## Known Constraints
- The product must not position itself as a medical device (regulatory risk)
- AI-generated carb estimates should be presented as guidance, not clinical precision
- The product must acknowledge it is a support tool, not a replacement for medical advice
