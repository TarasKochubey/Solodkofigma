# Material And Visual Language

Status: derived AI-generation context. Not canonical. Sources: `DESIGN_DNA.md`, `MATERIAL_SYSTEM.md`, `DESIGN_DNA.yaml`, `MATERIAL_TOKENS.yaml`, and Phase 06 design-system tokens.

## Visual Identity

Solodko is warm, atmospheric, object-centric precision nutrition software. It should feel calm, premium, and quietly intelligent. The category signal is lifestyle-quality health support, not medical utility, calorie tracker, fintech dashboard, or productivity app.

## Atmospheric Palette

The background is an organic time-of-day atmosphere:

- Morning: warm peach, soft cream, light sage.
- Afternoon: warm cream, warm neutral, very light sage.
- Evening: muted plum, soft slate, restrained indigo at the edge only.

The background should feel like light quality or weather. It should not read as a decorative gradient poster. Keep saturation restrained and movement nearly imperceptible.

## Material Hierarchy

Glass is selective.

Use frosted glass for:

- Meal cards.
- The orb.
- Active input composer.
- Active elevated objects.

Use soft translucent solids for:

- Log rows.
- Secondary list items.
- Tab bar surface.
- Utility settings rows.
- Quiet state surfaces.

Do not make every layer glass. If everything is glass, hierarchy collapses and the product becomes generic glassmorphism.

## Depth Hierarchy

- Z:0: atmospheric mesh background, the emotional sky.
- Z:1: floating recurring meal cards and content objects.
- Z:1 secondary: quiet soft-solid rows and supporting content.
- Z:2: active orb, active meal card, input composer, clarification.

Active objects elevate smoothly. Nothing should jump layers.

## Glass Quality

Primary glass should feel touchable:

- Translucent warm white.
- Soft blur around 32-40px for cards and active surfaces.
- Saturation boost so the background does not wash grey.
- 20-24px meal card radius.
- Inner edge highlight from upper-left.
- Soft acoustic shadow, never harsh.

Estimated or uncertain cards become softer and cooler. Exact and library cards feel more crisp and grounded.

## Typography

Typography carries hierarchy.

- Editorial serif: time-of-day anchors, emotional section moments.
- Operational sans: food names, carb values, labels, body, actions.
- Carb value: largest number on the card, highest contrast.
- Microcopy: one sentence maximum.
- Badges: small, quiet, pill-contained.

Avoid decorative color. Use scale, weight, spacing, and material.

## Card Layering

Cards are objects, not containers for dense data. They should have generous internal padding, no divider lines, and clear reading order. Recurring meal cards can float lightly. Active cards should feel more physically present.

Images may be used when they improve recognition, but they should not dominate the carb value or feel like stock food photography.

## Premium Softness

Premium here means restraint:

- Fewer elements.
- More breathing space.
- Softer surfaces.
- Clearer type.
- Calm motion.
- No urgency mechanics.

The product should look usable at a restaurant table, in sunlight, with one hand.

## Anti-Patterns

- Cold clinical white or grey dominance.
- Neon, saturated, or aggressive gradients.
- Generic frosted panels everywhere.
- Harsh shadows or high-contrast borders.
- Divider lines as structure.
- Red, orange, or green nutrition signaling.
- Dense tables, rings, charts, or quota visuals.
- Decorative illustrations replacing operational clarity.
