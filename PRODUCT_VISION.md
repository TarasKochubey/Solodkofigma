## Working Product Name
Solodko

## Product Category
Calm AI-native precision nutrition companion

## Vision Statement
Become the most calm and intelligent daily nutrition companion for people who count carbohydrates — replacing anxiety and guesswork at every meal with structured AI clarity, a growing personal food memory, and an experience that feels like a premium lifestyle app, not a medical utility.

## The Change This Product Creates
**Before:** A person who needs to count carbs at every meal opens a generic app, searches through a crowded database, manually estimates portions, struggles with homemade recipes, and still feels uncertain. Every meal is a small, stressful calculation.

**After:** They open Solodko, photograph, speak, or type what they're eating, and receive a calm, structured carb estimate in seconds — drawn from their personal food memory first, AI when needed. Logging a meal takes a few taps. The product feels like a calm, knowledgeable companion — not a medical calculator.

## Premium Promise
Speed + structured AI intelligence + warmth. Solodko is fast enough to use at the table, precise enough to meaningfully reduce carb-counting uncertainty, and warm enough that users genuinely want to open it every day.

## Recurring Meal Intelligence (Core Product Pillar)
The most important long-term mechanic in Solodko is not AI — it is memory.

Every meal logged builds a personal food universe that makes the next identical meal nearly frictionless. Recurring meals surface automatically by time of day. Breakfast staples appear in the morning. Common lunch dishes appear at midday. The home screen becomes a calm, intelligent hub that already knows what you probably need — without asking.

Over time, users do not search for their food. Their food finds them.

This compounding acceleration is the product's deepest moat:
- No competitor can replicate a user's personal food library
- Each logged meal is an investment that pays dividends forever
- The product becomes irreplaceable precisely because it becomes personal

The AI layer supports this system — filling gaps for unfamiliar foods, resolving ambiguity, scanning labels — but the memory system is the engine that makes Solodko feel irreplaceably yours.

## Home Screen Philosophy
The home screen is a calm, intelligent meal hub — not a dashboard.

It surfaces:
- Recurring meals at the right time of day
- Fast entry (type, speak, scan, photograph)
- A calm AI presence (orb or ambient anchor) as the interaction focus
- Minimal visual noise — breathing space, not information density

The AI orb or ambient character is:
- An emotional anchor — calming, not distracting
- The entry point for food input
- Quietly alive — present, responsive, never demanding

The home screen should feel like opening to something that already understands your day — and is ready to help without making you think.

## Platform
- iOS first
- Android: later phase
- Apple Watch: nice-to-have, later phase

## Non-Goals
- Not a general calorie-counting app for weight loss or fitness
- Not a full medical device or clinical dashboard
- Not a diabetes education platform
- Not a blood glucose monitor (though future CGM integration is a possibility)
- Not designed to replace medical advice

## Founder Emotional Intent
> "I want the app to convey calm and warmth, genuine help in the daily struggle."
> "People should forget about medical terms they already encounter every day."
> "It should feel almost like a lifestyle app."

## Assumptions
- The carb-counting friction is significant enough that users will pay for a better solution
- Premium design and AI speed together create a meaningful differentiation vs. existing medical and nutrition apps
- An iOS-first strategy reaches the highest-value segment of the target audience first

## Open Questions
- What onboarding experience best earns user trust and introduces AI-structured meal interaction?
- What is the right split between free tier and paid features?
- How do users respond to structured AI result cards vs. freeform AI answers — do they find them reassuring or limiting?
- How does the app introduce itself in the App Store — precision nutrition framing, or does it name diabetes explicitly?
