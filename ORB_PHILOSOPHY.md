# Orb Philosophy

Status: derived AI-generation context. Not canonical. Sources: `MOTION_LANGUAGE.md`, `MOTION_PHILOSOPHY.yaml`, `INTERACTION_PHILOSOPHY.yaml`, `DESIGN_DNA.md`, `HOME_SCREEN_DNA.yaml`, and `orb.yaml`.

## Emotional Role

The orb is the visible edge of Solodko's intelligence. It should feel alive enough to be trusted, but quiet enough to disappear from conscious attention. Its role is calm competence, not personality.

The orb should reduce tension during meal moments. It does this by staying present, readable, and restrained.

## Spatial Role

The orb is permanently anchored in the bottom-center console of the Home Hub. It is:

- The main input entry point.
- The system-state object.
- The spatial origin for meal result cards.
- The recovery anchor during offline, low-confidence, or clarification states.

It should not drift, become a floating action button, move into the header, or appear as a chat launcher.

## Material Role

The orb uses active frosted glass. It belongs at Z:2 when active and should feel more alive than secondary surfaces. Its inner glow should borrow from the time-of-day atmosphere:

- Morning: warm, peach-cream glow.
- Afternoon: soft warm neutral glow.
- Evening: muted plum or restrained cool glow.
- Low confidence: cool muted slate, never red.

## Interaction States

- Idle: slow breathing, scale 1.0 to 1.03, soft warm glow, 4000ms cycle.
- Listening: slight expansion to about 1.1, brighter glow, no frantic pulse.
- Processing: slow continuous rotation, shifting inner glow, 1200-1500ms loop.
- Clarification needed: rotation stops, orb dims, pulse slows, patient and expectant.
- Result ready: brief brightening, then shared-element expansion into the meal card.
- Low confidence / offline: slight deflation, muted cool glow, recovery actions nearby.

Reduced Motion: preserve meaning through opacity, stillness, material, and labels. Do not depend on rotation or morphing.

## Breathing Philosophy

Breathing is not decoration. It says the system is ready without demanding action. It must be slow enough to become ambient. If a user notices the animation as animation, it is probably too active.

The orb should never feel excited, impatient, celebratory, or disappointed.

## Ambient Intelligence

The orb does not talk about AI. It demonstrates AI through behavior:

- It waits quietly.
- It receives natural input.
- It processes without a spinner.
- It asks one missing question.
- It becomes the card when the answer is ready.
- It returns to idle when done.

The ideal impression: "the result appeared from the system I already trusted."

## Anti-Patterns

- Face, eyes, smile, mascot, assistant avatar, robot, or character.
- Generic plus-button FAB behavior.
- Microphone icon as the orb itself.
- Spinner inside or beside the orb.
- Red alarm state, shaking, buzzing, or failure haptic.
- Dramatic split, sparkle, reveal, explosion, or particle effect.
- Chat bubble launcher behavior.
- Moving the orb away from the bottom console.
